    Welcome to Pokédex Pro!

    The Pokédex is an electronic encyclopedia in the Pokémon world. It stores detailed information on all known Pokémon species.

    Features:

    • Search for any Pokémon by name or ID number
    • Get comprehensive information including stats, abilities, and moves
    • View official artwork and different sprite versions
    • Explore randomly featured Pokémon

    Usage:

    Simply type a Pokémon name or ID in the search box and click "Search" to find detailed information. Click on any Pokémon card to view its complete profile.

    Pro Tips:

    • Press Enter after typing a name to search quickly
    • Click on the Pokédex Pro title to view this help any time
    • Explore the Featured Pokémon section to discover new species
